Delete your We Learn account

We Learn by Bismark Nyota

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open We Learn and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email nyotabis@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my We Learn account". We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address, Google account ID) and display name
  • Learning progress, saved cards, chapter and quiz progress, daily activity and streak
  • Essays: photos, transcribed text, scores and feedback
  • AI tutor sessions and messages
  • Content reports you sent and AI usage counters

What is kept

Nothing linked to you stays in our database. Copies in routine backups expire within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone (such as your profile picture and cached content) is removed when you uninstall the app, or with Settings → Reset this phone.

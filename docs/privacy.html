We Learn

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

We Learn ("the app", "we") is a learning app made by Bismark Nyota. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. If you have questions, email nyotabis@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You can use the app offline without an account. Nothing leaves your phone until you sign in.
  • If you sign in with Google, we store your name, email address and learning progress so it is backed up and follows you to a new phone.
  • Essay photos and AI tutor messages are sent to our AI provider, DeepSeek, to be read and answered.
  • Screen time data is used only on your phone and is never uploaded.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. Your data is stored in Supabase's London (eu-west-2) region.
  • Google provides Google Sign-In. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • DeepSeek (an AI company based in China) processes essay photos, essay text and AI tutor messages to produce transcriptions, scores and replies. We do not send your name or email address to DeepSeek. DeepSeek's own privacy policy applies to how it handles this data.

News: the News tab loads public news feeds directly from publishers such as the BBC, The Herald, NewsDay, TechCrunch and IEEE Spectrum. Like any website, these publishers can see your IP address when the app downloads a feed or when you open an article.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you delete it, your account, progress, essays, essay photos and AI tutor history are deleted from our database immediately. Copies in routine backups expire within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Every table in our database uses row-level security, so each account can only read its own data. Essay photos are kept in a private storage bucket. AI provider keys are kept on our server and never in the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account: choose "Try offline first".
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. You can also request deletion without the app.
  • Permissions: notifications, camera, photos and usage access are all optional. The app works without them, and you can change them in your phone's settings.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

We Learn is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
